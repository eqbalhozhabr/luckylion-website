Monday, 9 A.M.

نوار زمان: اتاق در زمان‌های دیگر

فهرست اشیای اتاق (برای استفاده با صفحه‌کلید)

درباره‌ی این پرونده

Model No. 2. Orange Grove Court, Apartment 7.

Priya Raman, a ceramic artist aged 44, was found dead on her living-room floor at 07:52 on Sunday morning. She had moved into Apartment 7 six weeks earlier, after the divorce papers were drawn up. On Monday at 9 o'clock she was due to sign the settlement that splits the studio, the kiln and the money. Nothing is forced, nothing valuable is gone, and there are two cups on the table.

Four people have an account of their Saturday evening. Read the building's records, compare the room at different times, and say who did it, how, and why. People can lie. Meters, cameras and cups do not.
