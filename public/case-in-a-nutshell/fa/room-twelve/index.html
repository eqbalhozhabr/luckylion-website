Model No. 5. Hotel Meridian, Room 12.

Leonard Grey, 57, a hotel auditor, was found dead in bed in Room 12 at 07:10 on Sunday morning by the housekeeper, who had to use the override: the door was locked. A glass of water stood on the dresser, a bottle of sleeping pills lay empty, and his laptop was open. The police call it a suicide. His audit of the hotel's takings was due on Monday.

Four people had a reason to be near him that night. Read the lock, the cameras and the card system, check who could get where and when, and say who did it, how and why. Doors, cards and cameras keep time better than people.
