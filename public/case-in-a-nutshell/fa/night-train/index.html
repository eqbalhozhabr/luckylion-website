درباره‌ی این پرونده

Model No. 6. Night Express 417, Cabin 4.

Alma Voss, 61, an antiques appraiser, was found dead in her berth in Cabin 4 at 06:40 on Sunday morning by the conductor, who had to use the override: the door was bolted from inside. A cup stood on the dresser, her heart pills lay beside it, and her auction catalogue was nowhere to be seen. The doctor on board says her heart gave out. She was carrying a very valuable brooch to an auction on Monday.

Four people were near her that night. Read the lock, the platform camera, the receipts and the punch clock, and watch the timetable: the train only stops once. Say who did it, how and why.
