The Greenhouse

Case 4 · Difficulty 3 of 5 · about 28 min

About this case

Model No. 4. Aldermere House, the greenhouse.

Beatrix Aldermere, 74, orchid grower and head of the family, was found at 06:40 on Sunday morning at the foot of a stepladder in her greenhouse, beside a spilled watering can. She always checked the mist system at half past ten, so the family and the local police agree on a story: she climbed the ladder at 22:30 and fell. On Monday she was to sign a new will.

Four people were in the house or the grounds that night. Read the greenhouse's own records, test the family's stories against the room, and say who did it, how and why. People tell stories. Floors, doors and cameras do not.

How to play

  • Tap things in the room. Anything interesting has something to say.
  • The moments bar shows the room as witnesses saw it. A witness may lie, the room does not.
  • Each puzzle adds a fact to your notebook. Use the facts to say who did it, how, and why.
