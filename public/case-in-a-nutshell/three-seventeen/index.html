About this case

Model No. 1. Orange Grove Court, Apartment 4.

Elias Brandt, a retired clockmaker aged 71, was found dead in his bed at 07:05. The clock beside the bed had stopped at 3:17, so everyone assumed the old man had died in his sleep in the middle of the night.

You are a trainee detective and this model has been handed to you. Search the rooms, hear the witnesses, and say who did it, how, and why.

How to play

  • Tap things in the room. Anything interesting has something to say.
  • The moments bar shows the room as witnesses saw it. A witness may lie, the room does not.
  • Each puzzle adds a fact to your notebook. Use the facts to say who did it, how, and why.

Progress is saved on this device.
