Case in a Nutshell

Short murder cases in small pixel dioramas. Each case takes about 12 minutes, can be played on its own, and opens in your browser. Search the rooms, weigh the witnesses, and say who did it, how, and why.

Inspired by the 1940s crime-scene models built to train detectives. You are a trainee detective, and every case comes from the same building.

What is different here?

The goal is not to escape, it is to accuse. Puzzles do not hand you keys, they hand you truth: each one adds a fact to your notebook, and in the end you must prove who did it with those facts. Witnesses lie, the room does not.

Every case ends with a loose thread that leads to the next one.
