The Last Reading

Case 3 · Difficulty 3 of 5 · about 28 min

About this case

Model No. 3. Hartwell Library, the reading room.

Ingrid Solberg, 61, head librarian of Hartwell Library, was found dead on the reading-room floor at 22:05 on Saturday by the cleaner. The library was holding its last reading of the season in the Great Hall: a guest author, a full house and a hush you could hear. Nothing was taken, and the rare-book cabinet is locked.

Four people had reason to be near her tonight. Read the building's logs and recordings, compare the room at different times, and say who did it, how, and why. People talk. Logs and rooms do not lie.

How to play

  • Tap things in the room. Anything interesting has something to say.
  • The moments bar shows the room as witnesses saw it. A witness may lie, the room does not.
  • Each puzzle adds a fact to your notebook. Use the facts to say who did it, how, and why.
