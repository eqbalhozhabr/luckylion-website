In the wasteland, timing and resource management are everything. Once you collect resources from a card, you must place a Pickup Truck tile on it. This indicates that the location is depleted and you can no longer gather resources from that specific card.

While these trucks are a vital part of your strategy, they come with specific tactical rules you need to manage:

- **Limited Capacity:** Each pickup truck tile can store exactly one resource.
- **High Risk:** Resources left on pickup trucks are exposed. Opponents can steal them using a Plunder card.
- **No Safe Haven:** You cannot move resources from a pickup truck into your shelter's main storage.
- **Direct Spending:** Resources stored on these tiles can only be spent directly to pay for actions.
- **Restricted Zones:** Pickup truck tiles can never be placed on Monster Attack cards.

How much risk are you willing to take with your exposed resources?
