In The Last Shelter, every survivor brings a unique edge to the fight against the mist. Oscar, our seasoned explorer, exemplifies how a focused ability can redefine your entire strategy in a world of scarcity. Oscar possesses the unique ability to acquire water at a significantly reduced cost compared to other survivors. While other players might dedicate valuable actions or pay steep prices for this essential resource, Oscar's natural affinity for finding potable sources means he can often secure his water needs with less effort or fewer resources.

This isn't just a small bonus; it's a systemic shift in his resource priority, allowing him to free up other resources or actions for different crucial tasks like building or battling. Imagine a round where the 'Collect Water' Action Tile is on its Mist side, requiring a tough battle or a costly oil payment to flip. For most survivors, this might be a prohibitive obstacle, forcing them to delay water collection or take a risky fight.

But for Oscar, that very same 'Collect Water' action, even on the Mist side, might still be more efficient than another player's Fire-side 'Collect Water' action due to his inherent cost reduction. He might even choose to take the Mist-side battle, knowing he has a buffer from his resource savings elsewhere, allowing him to bank a Trap Tile if he fails, turning a disadvantage into a long-term asset. This tactical flexibility is a hallmark of Oscar's playstyle.

When every drop of water is precious, how would you leverage Oscar's unique advantage to secure your shelter, knowing you can bypass the usual struggles for this vital resource?
